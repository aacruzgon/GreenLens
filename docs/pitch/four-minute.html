The vision models on our home GPU rig work on the photos. The job and its events live in SurrealDB, and each stage streams to the phone, even to the Dynamic Island. For a small farm, Claude searches the crew's Slack reports and the Google Drive spray log, and cites both. We tested it on Era by Eon's simulators: about 120 Slack messages over 180 days, with a planted prompt injection. 54 of 54 answers right, 18 questions run three times each, and the injection was never followed.

Plant pictures are drawn stand-ins for real photos. Screen content follows screens.md 3.1 to 3.4 and its wording rules (section 6): the diagnosis never shows a confidence number, bands appear only on findings and measurements, and status is always written as a word. The 3D garden is built only from the garden map's bed outlines, plant pins and measured sizes (architecture 6.8); no camera scan of the yard is used. three.js r128 loads from cdnjs for the 3D view.

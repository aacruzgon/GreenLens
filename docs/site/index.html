Capture

A camera that knows what to ask for.

Capture works like the Camera app. The shots are its modes: whole plant, close-up, underside, soil, fruit. A card drops in with each shot's instruction, the distance reads live, and a dial around the shutter fills one segment per needed photo.

Under the hoodA Swift module in Expo

Our LiDAR module: the camera with depth, the mode bar as iOS 26's segmented control on glass, and an ARKit session that maps the garden.

app/modules/lidar · 12 Swift files · LidarCameraView · ModePicker

Live analysis

Watch the rig think.

The photos go to a GPU rig at home. Each stage streams back as it runs, and findings land on the photo as chips. When two problems look alike, it asks you one question with picture answers instead of guessing. A Live Activity is built to carry the scan to the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island.

Under the hoodSurrealDB, streamed

Every stage is an event with a gap-free sequence number in SurrealDB 3.3.4. Drop the connection and the app resumes from the last one it saw. Workers on the rig pull their jobs, so the rig opens no ports.

API killed mid-scan on the rig: events 1–61, no gap

Diagnosis

A name, and what it rests on.

The photo fades into a ground tinted from it, with the diagnosis centred over its edge and what it rests on: three photos, your answer and the weather. No confidence number, by design. The risk of spread, a helpful ladybug larva and the plan follow.

Under the hoodVision makes facts

BioCLIP 2 names the species, our disease head ranks the problems, and Qwen3.5-9B checks the telltale signs. Fixed rules set the risk. The language model only writes the words.

6 photos and 40 crops read in 1.2 s on an RTX 3070

The plan, step by step

One step per screen.

Each step says when, how and why, with its source. A step that repeats turns into dated tasks the moment you finish the one before it.

Under the hoodClaude writes the plan

Claude Opus 5.5 turns the evidence into steps through strict structured output, and every step must cite a source. A dose check keeps amounts out of plans and chat. They come from the label.

claude-opus-5-5 · output_config.format · cited steps · dose check

Chat

Answers you can use, not only read.

Ask about the frost. Claude checks the forecast and which plants are tender, then answers with a frost-cover planner you can toggle. One tap adds a task for each plant you chose.

Under the hoodClaude, with 21 tools

Answer blocks are built from the tools' records, never from the model's arithmetic. Every task or plan change it proposes waits for your confirmation.

Memory and farm records

It remembers, and it checks your notes.

Say "no copper" once and it stays said. Ask when the east bed was last sprayed, and the answer cites your crew's spray log in Google Drive and this week's report in Slack.

Under the hoodSurrealDB Agent Memory

A memory shared with the garden is mirrored as a belief. A newer one supersedes it, and deleting it forgets it there too. Private memories never leave our database.

How it works

A phone, a server, and a GPU rig at home.

The app talks to one Rust API. The API keeps everything in SurrealDB and RustFS and runs no GPU model itself: workers on the rig pull jobs, run the vision models and the vision-language model, and post results back. If no worker claims a job in 15 seconds, Claude takes it. Claude also writes the plan and answers in the chat.

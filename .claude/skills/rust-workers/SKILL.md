# The `worker` binary

How to write and review the `worker` binary: one binary started with `--caps perception`, `--caps vlm` or `--caps fallback` ([architecture 3, Rust crates](../../../docs/architecture/README.md#rust-crates)), that pulls jobs from the API, runs them, and reports back. Versions: Rust 1.99 (edition 2024), tokio 1.53.3, tokio-util 0.7.20, reqwest 0.13.5, ort 2.0.0-rc.14 (`load-dynamic`, `api-29`), ndarray 0.17.2 ([versions.md](../rust/versions.md)). Its design is [backend worker](../../../docs/backend/worker.md), with the perception models in [backend vision](../../../docs/backend/vision.md) and the HTTP clients in [backend integrations](../../../docs/backend/integrations.md); this skill follows them, and where they differ the documents win.

**The project documents win**, and [contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#5-worker-api-and-jobs) wins over everything else: [leases, runs and attempts](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts), [heartbeat](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internalworkersheartbeat), [claim](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsclaim), [payloads](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#payloads), [events](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsidevents), [complete](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsidcomplete) and [results](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#results). Also: [contracts 1, versioning](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#versioning) and [authentication headers](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#authentication-headers), [contracts 2](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#2-errors) (the `409 lease_lost` body), [contracts 6](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#6-vlm-structured-output) (what goes to the VLM), [architecture 8.4](../../../docs/architecture/README.md#84-job-leasing-and-fallback), [architecture 10](../../../docs/architecture/README.md#10-models-and-gpu-placement) and [storage 3](../../../docs/storage/README.md#3-how-images-are-served). When this skill or a crate disagrees with them, follow the documents and report the disagreement.

## 1. Shape of the process

Created **once at start**, never per job:

| Thing | Shared how | Source |
|---|---|---|
| One `reqwest::Client`, from `integrations::http::client` with a 600 s read gap | Cloned into `HttpWorkerApi` and the VLM or LLM client; the clones share one connection pool | [Client](https://docs.rs/reqwest/0.13.5/reqwest/struct.Client.html), [backend worker 1, HTTP timeouts](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#1-purpose-and-boundaries) |
| A `Semaphore` with `slots` permits | The claim loop takes a permit before each claim; the job task holds it | [contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts) |
| A `CpuPool` for CPU work | Cloned into tasks; a `Semaphore` in front of every `spawn_blocking` (section 3) | [rust-async, blocking 3](../rust-async/blocking.md#3-bounded-cpu-work-in-api), [spawn_blocking](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/task/fn.spawn_blocking.html) |
| The model, `Arc<dyn VisionModel>`, chosen by `--caps` and loaded before the first claim | Shared by every job task | [backend worker 4](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#4-configuration), `build_model` |
| The ort `Environment` and every `Session` (perception only) | Owned by the inference thread ([ort.md](ort.md)) | [architecture 10](../../../docs/architecture/README.md#10-models-and-gpu-placement), [backend vision 6](../../../docs/backend/vision.md#6-perception) |
| The running-jobs registry | Read by the heartbeat, written by job tasks | [loop.md](loop.md) |

Tasks: the **heartbeat task**, the **claim loop**, and **one task per job** spawned on a `TaskTracker` ([TaskTracker](https://docs.rs/tokio-util/0.7.20/tokio_util/task/task_tracker/struct.TaskTracker.html)). Tokens: `shutdown` (the process token of [rust-async](../rust-async/shutdown.md#6-worker)) stops the claim loop; `stop_heartbeat` is separate and cancelled only after the drain, because draining jobs still need their leases extended ([contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts)); `abandon` is the parent of every job's token and is cancelled when the drain runs out of time. Token semantics: [rust-async, cancellation](../rust-async/cancellation.md#3-cancellationtoken).

## 2. The loop

Each rule is implemented and tested in [loop.md](loop.md).

1. **Heartbeat every 10 s on its own task**, listing every held job with its lease token in `current_jobs`. A job left out stops being extended and is requeued when its 2-minute lease expires. The registry entry is removed by a drop guard when the job task ends, however it ends. [contracts 5, heartbeat](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internalworkersheartbeat), [leases](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts)
2. **`MissedTickBehavior::Delay` on the heartbeat `interval`.** After a heartbeat call that outlasted the 10 s period, `Delay` sends one catch-up heartbeat and then spaces them 10 s from it. The default, `Burst`, fires every missed tick back to back, each a copy of the last. Give each heartbeat call a 5 s timeout, far below the 30 s after which the broker requeues all of the worker's jobs; on failure, log and keep ticking. [MissedTickBehavior](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/time/enum.MissedTickBehavior.html), [interval](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/time/fn.interval.html), [contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts)

```rust
use std::time::Duration;

use tokio::time::{Instant, MissedTickBehavior, interval};

/// Ticks seen in the 4 s after a heartbeat call that took 25 s.
async fn ticks_after_a_slow_call(behavior: MissedTickBehavior) -> usize {
    let mut every = interval(Duration::from_secs(10));
    every.set_missed_tick_behavior(behavior);
    every.tick().await; // the first tick is immediate
    tokio::time::sleep(Duration::from_secs(25)).await; // the slow heartbeat
    let until = Instant::now() + Duration::from_secs(4);
    let mut ticks = 0;
    while tokio::time::timeout_at(until, every.tick()).await.is_ok() {
        ticks += 1;
    }
    ticks
}

#[tokio::test(start_paused = true)]
async fn delay_sends_one_catch_up_heartbeat_burst_sends_several() {
    assert_eq!(ticks_after_a_slow_call(MissedTickBehavior::Delay).await, 1);
    assert_eq!(ticks_after_a_slow_call(MissedTickBehavior::Burst).await, 2);
}
```

3. **`cancel_job_ids` cancels that job's `CancellationToken`, and the job stops and sends nothing more**: any later call about the job gets `409 lease_lost`. **Cancellation is by drop**: the runner (the `VisionModel`) gets no token; `run_job` `select!`s on the token and the runner's future and, when the token fires, returns and drops the future. Each adapter makes the drop effective: the perception runner's inference wait holds a `TerminateOnDrop` guard that calls `RunOptions::terminate`, and a dropped VLM call closes its connection ([ort.md, the inference thread](ort.md#4-the-inference-thread)). [backend domain 9, models](../../../docs/backend/domain.md#models), [backend worker 7](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#7-cancellation), [contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts), [child_token](https://docs.rs/tokio-util/0.7.20/tokio_util/sync/struct.CancellationToken.html#method.child_token), [RunOptions::terminate](https://docs.rs/ort/2.0.0-rc.14/ort/session/struct.RunOptions.html#method.terminate)
4. **Take a slot before claiming.** `slots.clone().acquire_owned()` first, then the claim; the `OwnedSemaphorePermit` moves into the job task and is dropped when it ends. The worker then never holds more than `slots` jobs and never polls while full; the broker enforces the same limit with `204`. Send the same `slots` in the claim and the heartbeat. [acquire_owned](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/sync/struct.Semaphore.html#method.acquire_owned), [contracts 5, claim](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsclaim)
5. **Claim is a long poll**: `wait_s: 25` (the maximum is 30), with a request timeout longer than the poll (`wait_s + 10 s`). A `204` after the server waited means nothing arrived: claim again at once. The API answers `204` only at the poll's deadline or when it shuts down ([backend api 15](../../../docs/backend/api.md#15-the-job-broker-and-the-worker-api), `claim`), and the shutdown one can't turn into a spin: the listener is gone, so the next claim fails to connect (or gets Caddy's `502`) and backs off as an error ([backend worker 5](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#5-the-lifecycle-state-machine), probed there). The worker in worker 5 therefore claims again at once after every `204`. This skill adds one defensive guard: a `204` that comes back in under half of `wait_s` backs off like an error (rule 12), so no `204` path can ever spin. It is *this skill's choice* and still a difference from [backend worker 5](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#5-the-lifecycle-state-machine), which claims again at once on the strength of its shutdown probe; the guard changes nothing while the API behaves as that probe showed. The test `an_early_204_backs_off_instead_of_spinning` in [loop.md](loop.md) shows 6 to 8 claims in 60 s. [contracts 5, claim](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsclaim), [reqwest.md](reqwest.md#2-timeouts)
6. **Every call about a job sends `X-Lease-Token`** with the job's `lease_token`, next to `Authorization: Bearer <worker token>`. [contracts 1, authentication headers](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#authentication-headers)
7. **`409 lease_lost` from any call drops the job silently**: no `complete`, no more events, the slot is freed. Only the body's `code` says `lease_lost`, so read it before treating the status as an error ([reqwest.md, reading errors](reqwest.md#4-reading-errors)). The job's progress sink cancels the job's token on `lease_lost` from `events`, so the job task stops at once even if the model goes on ([backend worker 1, 7](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#7-cancellation); `Progress` in [loop.md](loop.md)). [contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts), [contracts 2](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#2-errors)
8. **One `partial_result` per finished stage**, sent after the stage's last batch. A failed `events` call for any other reason is logged and the job goes on (`Progress::send` in [loop.md](loop.md)): `complete` repeats every stage, and the API keeps the `complete` version. A late batch that reaches the API after `complete` with the same token gets `204` and is dropped there ([backend api 15](../../../docs/backend/api.md#15-the-job-broker-and-the-worker-api)). [contracts 5, events](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsidevents), [results](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#results)
9. **`complete` carries `status` `done` with `result`, or `failed` with `error: { code, message, retryable }`, and always `model_versions` and `timings_ms`.** After a timeout, a connection error, or an error body with `retryable: true` (or a `429` or `5xx` without one), send the same body again with backoff, waiting at least the body's `retry_after_s` when it has one: a repeat for a job that already completed returns `204` and changes nothing. An error body with `retryable: false` is not retried in a loop ([reqwest.md, reading errors](reqwest.md#4-reading-errors), [contracts 2](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#2-errors)). The body's `retryable` decides whatever the status, as contracts 2 defines it ("the same request may succeed later"): a `429 rate_limited` with `retry_after_s` is retried after that wait, while a `500 internal` or a `429 budget_exceeded` (`retryable: false`) is logged and not retried; only an answer without an error body falls back to the status (a `429` or `5xx` is retried, any other `4xx` is not). This is [backend worker 6](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#6-leases-heartbeats-and-completion)'s table, "What an answer means". Stop retrying once `max_runtime_s` has passed; the lease can't outlive it. [contracts 5, complete](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsidcomplete), [leases](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts)
10. **`v != 1`, or a `v: 1` job this build can't read, fails with `unsupported_version`**, `retryable: false`, without running it. Decode `v`, `job_id` and `lease_token` before the payload with `decode_claim` ([rust-contract-types, the worker checks v first](../rust-contract-types/unions.md#3-the-worker-checks-v-first), region `JobHead`): another `v`, an unknown `kind` or a payload that doesn't decode all come back as `ClaimedJob::Unsupported(head)`, and the head's lease token completes the job. The claim filters by the worker's kinds, so such a job is an older worker against a newer API; failing it at once beats leaving it to expire, and a newer worker gets it after a requeue. A body whose head doesn't decode can't be completed at all: log it and let the lease expire. Settled in [contracts 12, questions 19 and 21](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#12-open-questions) and stated in [contracts 5, leases](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts) and [complete](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsidcomplete) ([backend worker 16](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#16-decisions-on-the-contracts-open-questions)). [contracts 1, versioning](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#versioning)
11. **Stop a job before its `max_runtime_s`.** Take the deadline from the job's own `max_runtime_s`, not from a table in the worker (the limits are doubled on the fallback worker), measured from receipt on the monotonic clock, minus 15 s for `complete`. Past it, the runner's future is dropped (its drop guard terminates an ONNX run) and the job fails with `max_runtime_exceeded`, `retryable: true` (settled in [contracts 12, question 18](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#12-open-questions); [contracts 5, complete](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsidcomplete)). Don't compare `lease_until` with the local clock: it is the server's time. The broker enforces `max_runtime` regardless, so a hung model loses the job either way. [contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts), [timeout_at](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/time/fn.timeout_at.html), [tokio Instant](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/time/struct.Instant.html)
12. **Back off when the API is unreachable**: 1 s doubling to a 30 s cap, each delay drawn from `[d/2, d]` so it is never near zero; reset after any answer that isn't an early `204`. When the API sent `retry_after_s`, wait at least that long. Never retry in a tight loop. *Design choice for this repo*; the tests in [loop.md](loop.md) show at most 8 claims in 60 s, and a retry 7 s after a `retry_after_s: 7`. [contracts 2](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#2-errors), [rand::random_range](https://docs.rs/rand/0.10.3/rand/fn.random_range.html)
13. **SIGTERM or Ctrl-C: stop claiming, drain, then stop heartbeating.** The claim loop `select!`s on `shutdown` with `biased;` and drops an open long poll. Running jobs get `DRAIN_SECONDS` (20 s) to finish. Jobs still running then are abandoned: their tokens are cancelled, their runners dropped and nothing is sent, so the broker requeues them 30 s after the last heartbeat; a job's first two requeues don't count as attempts. There is no call to hand a lease back, and a claim dropped at shutdown that the server had already granted is requeued the same way (settled in [contracts 12, question 20](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#12-open-questions); [contracts 5, leases](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts)). Docker Compose sends SIGKILL `stop_grace_period` after SIGTERM, 10 s by default, so every worker service sets `stop_grace_period: 30s` ([backend worker 12](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#12-deployment)); `infra/` doesn't exist yet. **The whole shutdown ends within `DRAIN_SECONDS`**: at the bound every await in a job task selects on the job's token, a `complete` call in flight included, and `stop_heartbeat` drops a heartbeat call in flight. Without those two cuts, a `complete` in flight at the bound (10 s deadline) and then a heartbeat (5 s) would run on: 20 + 10 + 5 = 35 s, past the 30 s grace. A `complete` cut at the bound is either committed by the API or the job is requeued 30 s after the last heartbeat. The grace's 10 s above the drain is left for blocking work already started and for Docker ([backend worker 5](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#5-the-lifecycle-state-machine), "Shutdown time"; [12](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#12-deployment)); the test `the_whole_shutdown_ends_within_the_drain_bound` in [loop.md](loop.md) shows exactly 20 s. [Compose file: stop_grace_period](https://docs.docker.com/reference/compose-file/services/#stop_grace_period), [rust-async, shutdown](../rust-async/shutdown.md#6-worker), [ctrl_c](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/signal/fn.ctrl_c.html)
14. **A blocking call outlives its task.** Dropping the future of a `spawn_blocking` task doesn't stop the closure, and dropping the runtime waits for every started blocking task. Use `RunOptions::terminate` for ONNX Runtime ([ort.md](ort.md)) and keep other blocking work short. [spawn_blocking](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/task/fn.spawn_blocking.html), [Runtime::shutdown_timeout](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/runtime/struct.Runtime.html#method.shutdown_timeout)

## 3. Slots, pools and backpressure

| `--caps`, card | `slots` | Where the heavy work runs | What bounds it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `perception`, RTX 3070 | 1. Batches inside the job | ONNX Runtime with CUDA, on the inference thread | One job; each model runs once per job, in batches of at most `PERCEPTION_MAX_BATCH` (default 16) and its own cap |
| `vlm`, RTX 5070 Ti | Its own `VLM_SLOTS`, 1 or 2; vLLM's `--max-num-seqs` is the same value | Its vLLM sidecar, over HTTP | The slots, the call's 240 s deadline and the job's soft deadline |
| `vlm`, RX 9070 XT | Its own `VLM_SLOTS`, 1 or 2, from its own benchmark on its own server; `--max-num-seqs` (vLLM) or `-np` (llama.cpp) is the same value | Its sidecar: vLLM on ROCm, or llama.cpp with Vulkan | As the 5070 Ti. With `VLM_SLOTS=1` a slower card takes the overflow; the two workers race for jobs and the claim gives each job to one |
| `fallback`, control plane | `FALLBACK_SLOTS`, default 4 | The LLM API, over HTTPS | The slots, set from the LLM API's rate limit |

Every VLM worker is its own process with its own `WORKER_ID` and settings, and all serve the same checkpoint ([contracts 5, leases](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts), the `slots` table; [backend worker 11](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#11-multi-vendor-gpus)). Batching: [contracts 5, results](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#results).

- **Not in the contracts yet:** each card's `VLM_SLOTS` and `PERCEPTION_MAX_BATCH` come from the day-2 benchmark ([contracts 12, questions 1 and 9](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#12-open-questions)), and the RX 9070 XT's server, vLLM on ROCm or llama.cpp with Vulkan, from the day-1 and day-2 checks ([question 25](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#12-open-questions); [backend worker 11](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#11-multi-vendor-gpus)).

- **No unbounded queue anywhere.** Work enters through a slot permit, a CPU permit, or a bounded channel (`mpsc::channel(1)` to the inference thread). Never `unbounded_channel` or a `spawn` per image without a permit. [mpsc::channel](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/sync/mpsc/fn.channel.html)
- **CPU work (decode, resize to `VLM_MAX_PIXELS`) goes through rust-async's `CpuPool`**: a semaphore in front of `spawn_blocking`, with its tests ([rust-async, blocking 3](../rust-async/blocking.md#3-bounded-cpu-work-in-api)). tokio's blocking pool grows up to a very large limit and then queues; its documentation says to bound CPU-bound work with a semaphore ([spawn_blocking](https://docs.rs/tokio/1.53.3/tokio/task/fn.spawn_blocking.html)). Size the pool below the core count so the runtime and ONNX Runtime's pool keep some (*design choice*). On the perception worker too, decoding and pre-processing run on the blocking pool behind the semaphore (`CPU_PERMITS`, default 2), and only tensors go to the inference thread ([backend vision 6](../../../docs/backend/vision.md#6-perception); [backend worker 4](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#4-configuration); [rust-async, blocking 4](../rust-async/blocking.md#4-the-perception-worker-and-onnx-runtimes-threads)). [contracts 6, image preparation](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#image-preparation)

## 4. reqwest 0.13.5 in brief

Details, sources and tests: [reqwest.md](reqwest.md).

- One `Client` per process, built with `Client::builder()` so a TLS set-up failure is an error, not a panic; in the worker that is `integrations::http::client`, cloned into every client. [reqwest.md, the client](reqwest.md#1-the-client)
- 0.13 changed the TLS default to rustls with the platform verifier. On Linux, `build()` fails when the image has no CA certificates (read in the source, not run), so every worker image installs them ([backend worker 12](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#12-deployment)). [reqwest.md, the client](reqwest.md#1-the-client)
- `timeout` is the whole request, `connect_timeout` only the connect, and `read_timeout` each read, **including the wait for the response headers**. No short `read_timeout` on the shared client: it would kill the claim long poll and every non-streamed VLM call. `integrations`' shared builder defaults to 60 s; the worker overrides it to **600 s**, above the VLM call's 240 s deadline, and every call sets its own `timeout`, which overrides the client's ([backend worker 1, HTTP timeouts](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#1-purpose-and-boundaries); [backend integrations 4](../../../docs/backend/integrations.md#4-shared-http-client-retries-pacing-sse)). [reqwest.md, timeouts](reqwest.md#2-timeouts)
- Read the error body of every non-2xx answer before anything else: `error_for_status` would consume it, and only the body has `lease_lost`, `retryable` and `retry_after_s`. A body that couldn't be read is a network error, not a bad body, although reqwest calls both `is_decode()`. [reqwest.md, reading errors](reqwest.md#4-reading-errors)

## 5. ort 2.0.0-rc.14 in brief

Details, sources and the compiled examples: [ort.md](ort.md).

- **`load-dynamic` with `api-29`, default features off**: nothing is linked at build time; one binary opens Microsoft's ONNX Runtime 1.31 or AMD's 1.29 MIGraphX build from `ORT_DYLIB_PATH`, with `ort::init_from` at start so a missing library is an error, not a panic. [ort.md, environment](ort.md#1-environment-and-execution-providers), [backend vision 2](../../../docs/backend/vision.md#2-dependencies)
- **The execution provider is a cargo feature (`cuda`, `migraphx`, `webgpu`) chosen at run time by `ORT_EP`**; one that isn't compiled in fails start-up. CUDA and MIGraphX can't share one `download-binaries` build, which is one reason the design loads dynamically. [ort.md, environment](ort.md#1-environment-and-execution-providers), [CUDA on the rig](ort.md#2-cuda-on-the-rig), [backend worker 11](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#11-multi-vendor-gpus)
- `ort::init_from(…)…build()` once per process (a second `build()` returns an error), then `Session::builder(&env)` for each model at start. A provider that fails to register is logged and skipped, and the session runs on the CPU: add `.error_on_failure()` so the perception worker refuses to start instead. [ort.md, environment](ort.md#1-environment-and-execution-providers)
- The perception image is the CUDA 13 runtime with cuDNN 9 and ONNX Runtime 1.31's libraries; the host's one NVIDIA driver serves both NVIDIA cards. [ort.md, CUDA on the rig](ort.md#2-cuda-on-the-rig)
- `Session::run` takes `&mut self` in rc.14. The perception worker's inference thread owns its sessions, so no `Mutex` is needed; `api`'s CPU models sit in a session pool instead ([backend vision 4](../../../docs/backend/vision.md#4-onnx-runtime-environment-execution-providers-session-pool), [6](../../../docs/backend/vision.md#6-perception)). One global intra-op pool for all sessions, with spinning off. Never run inference on a runtime worker thread. [ort.md, sessions and threads](ort.md#3-sessions-and-threads)
- `RunOptions::terminate` stops a run from another thread. The inference wait holds a drop guard that calls it when the wait is dropped, which is how a cancelled job or a soft deadline stops a run. [ort.md, the inference thread](ort.md#4-the-inference-thread)
- Examples and tests may use `ort` and link, but never build a session ([rust, checking the examples](../rust/SKILL.md#checking-the-examples)).

## 6. Images and the VLM

- **Fetch images from each `JobImage`'s `url`** (`GET /internal/jobs/{job_id}/images/{image_id}`), with the bearer token and `X-Lease-Token`, and only from the API's own origin ([reqwest.md, reading errors](reqwest.md#4-reading-errors)). Each fetch has a 30 s deadline ([backend worker 1, HTTP timeouts](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#1-purpose-and-boundaries)) and is retried twice after an `Unavailable` answer; one that still fails completes the job with `image_fetch_failed`, `retryable: true` ([backend worker 6](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#6-leases-heartbeats-and-completion)). Only the images in the payload exist for the worker: payloads never list depth maps, and no route serves one. A `409 lease_lost` here drops the job like any other call. [storage 3](../../../docs/storage/README.md#3-how-images-are-served), [contracts 5, payloads](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#payloads), [contracts 1, wire format](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#wire-format)
- **The VLM call goes through `integrations::openai_compat`** (the fallback's through `integrations::anthropic`), over the shared client: a 240 s deadline per call (300 s on the fallback), no client retries, and the job's soft deadline around the whole model. The fallback reads the hosted model's key from `LLM_API_KEY`, like `api` ([reqwest.md, the VLM sidecar](reqwest.md#5-the-vlm-sidecar); [backend worker 1, HTTP timeouts](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#1-purpose-and-boundaries); [backend worker 4](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#4-configuration)). The prompt, the per-call JSON Schema and the decoding of the answer follow [contracts 6](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#6-vlm-structured-output) and [backend worker 8–9](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#8-the-vlm-adapters); they are not this skill's subject.

## 7. Settled and open

Settled in [contracts 12](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#12-open-questions) (v0.10) and now in [contracts 5](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#5-worker-api-and-jobs) ([backend worker 16](../../../docs/backend/worker.md#16-decisions-on-the-contracts-open-questions)):

- **Job error codes (question 18)**, `domain::jobs::JobErrorCode` ([rust-errors, jobs](../rust-errors/jobs.md); [contracts 5, complete](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internaljobsidcomplete)): `unsupported_version` and `unknown_label_version`, not retryable; `model_error`, retryable except when the adapter can't do the job's kind; `image_fetch_failed` and `max_runtime_exceeded`, retryable. A code the API doesn't know is kept as `Other(String)`; a `done` result that doesn't decode for the job's kind is stored as `model_error`, retryable.
- **`unsupported_version` is not retryable (question 19)** (rule 10).
- **No lease hand-back at shutdown (question 20)**: a lease the server granted to a dropped claim is requeued when the heartbeat is 30 s stale or the lease expires (rule 13; [contracts 5, leases](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#leases-runs-and-attempts)).
- **An unknown `kind` (question 21)**: the claim filters by the worker's kinds; one that slips through, or a payload this build can't read, is completed `failed` with `unsupported_version`, not retryable (rule 10).

Still open:

- `VLM_SLOTS`, the batch caps and the RX 9070 XT's server: [section 3](#3-slots-pools-and-backpressure) (questions 1, 9 and 25).
- **Not in the contracts yet:** whether llama.cpp frees a slot when a cancelled call's connection drops ([contracts 12, question 33](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#12-open-questions); [reqwest.md, the VLM sidecar](reqwest.md#5-the-vlm-sidecar)).
- **The heartbeat's `gpu` field** is optional ([contracts 5, heartbeat](../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#post-internalworkersheartbeat)); leave it out until something measures VRAM (no crate for it is pinned).

## 8. Review checklist

- [ ] One `reqwest::Client` (600 s read gap, no total timeout), one ort `Environment`, the model and every `Session` created at start; nothing loaded per job.
- [ ] Heartbeat on its own task, every 10 s, `MissedTickBehavior::Delay`, a per-call timeout well under 30 s, and every held job in `current_jobs` with its lease token.
- [ ] SIGTERM and Ctrl-C both start the drain; the heartbeat keeps running while jobs drain; its token is not a child of `shutdown`; no `complete` or heartbeat call in flight outlasts the drain; the worker's compose service sets `stop_grace_period: 30s` (rule 13).
- [ ] `cancel_job_ids` cancels the job's token, and the job task drops the runner's future at once; the runner takes no token, and every adapter makes the drop effective (rule 3).
- [ ] The progress sink cancels the job's token on `409 lease_lost` (rule 7).
- [ ] A slot permit is taken before each claim and held by the job task; `slots` is the same in claim and heartbeat; each VLM worker has its own `VLM_SLOTS` (section 3).
- [ ] Claim `wait_s` ≤ 30 with a request timeout longer than it; a `204` after the full wait loops at once, an early `204` backs off; no short client-wide `read_timeout` (rule 5).
- [ ] `X-Lease-Token` on events, complete and image calls.
- [ ] Every error body is read before anything else; `lease_lost` drops the job without `complete`; `retryable` and `retry_after_s` decide the retry; a bad JSON body and a body that couldn't be read are told apart (rules 7 and 9).
- [ ] `complete` has `v`, `status`, `model_versions` and `timings_ms`, and is retried with backoff (at least `retry_after_s`) after network errors and retryable error bodies, until `max_runtime_s` (rule 9).
- [ ] Another `v`, an unknown `kind` and an unreadable payload all fail with `unsupported_version`, `retryable: false`, before any work, using the head's lease token (rule 10).
- [ ] The job stops itself before `max_runtime_s`, on the monotonic clock, with `max_runtime_exceeded` (rule 11).
- [ ] Every `JobError.code` is a `JobErrorCode`, its `retryable` the contract's, its message the error's chain ([rust-errors, jobs](../rust-errors/jobs.md)).
- [ ] Every retry loop sleeps with backoff; none can spin, not even on a `204` (rules 5 and 12).
- [ ] CPU work goes through the `CpuPool`; no unbounded channel or queue (section 3).
- [ ] Inference never runs on a runtime worker thread; a dropped inference wait reaches `RunOptions::terminate` ([ort.md](ort.md#4-the-inference-thread)).
- [ ] `ORT_EP` picks a compiled-in provider, registered with `error_on_failure` ([ort.md](ort.md#1-environment-and-execution-providers)).
- [ ] The VLM and LLM calls go through `integrations`, with their own deadlines and `RetryPolicy::NONE`; the hosted key is `LLM_API_KEY` (section 6).
- [ ] Images come only from each `JobImage.url` on the API's origin; nothing reads depth (section 6).

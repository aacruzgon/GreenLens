# Face check: faces-2026-10-22

93 Open Images V7 photos (validation and test splits) of people near plants, with 313 annotated faces (`ml/training/data/splits/faces_openimages.tsv`), and the 152 photos of the dev cases (`ml/training/eval/manifest.jsonl`). Each went through `vision`'s `face_check` example: `decode_upload`, the quality gate, then `FaceDetector::detect` (YuNet 2026may on the CPU, the whole image at 1,280 px and 1,280 px tiles of the full image at stride 960) with `FaceConfig::default()` but the threshold, as three uploads: the photo as it is (`original`), and at most 3,200 and 2,048 px on the long side (Lanczos, JPEG quality 85).

A face counts when it is 20 px or more across (its box's width) in the stored image (1,600 px long side). It is caught when the detected boxes, grown 25% on each side as `api` grows them, cover 90% or more of its box ([evaluation 4](../../../../docs/vision/evaluation.md#upload-and-privacy-control-plane)). Target: 98%.

**At the shipped threshold 0.3: original 94.7% (267/282), 3200 95.0% (268/282), 2048 94.0% (265/282). Dev plant photos with a face box: original 89/152, 3200 88/152, 2048 85/152.**

## What it shows

Written after looking at every missed face (all three sizes), all 510 boxes on the dev plant photos (11 sheets) and a sample of the boxes on no annotated face, in `ml/training/runs/faces/crops/`.

**Both targets fail at the shipped settings.** Recall is 94.7% (267 of 282 faces) against 98%. 89 of the 152 dev plant photos (58.6%) get at least one face box against 2%, and the blur covers 11.5% of an average dev plant photo, up to 85% of one ("Changes weighed", below). No threshold meets both: at 0.15 recall is 97.2% with 78.3% of the plant photos blurred; at 0.8, 5.3% of them are still blurred and recall falls to 80.5%.

**The false boxes are YuNet's, not the port's.** On the same crops, the Rust detector gives the same boxes and scores as OpenCV 4.12's `FaceDetectorYN` on the same model file: 51 boxes on a 1,153 × 1,280 crop of a grape leaf and 4 on a crop of a group photo, every box and score equal to 4 decimals (✓ ran 2026-10-22, `opencv-python-headless` 4.12.0.88).

**The 510 boxes on the dev plant photos:** none is a face. They are leaf spots and lesions (72 on one grape leaf), maize leaf streaks, apples and tomatoes, stems and buds, mulch and soil, spider mites, webs, leaf undersides, and fingertips holding a leaf (3 photos). The garden photos get most of them: 78 of the 87 iNaturalist photos have a box, against 11 of the 65 field-set photos, mostly single leaves on a plain background.

**The 15 faces missed on the originals at 0.3:**

- **4 blurred, but under the 90% rule** (83–90% of the box covered). Open Images boxes the whole head, hair and ears included; YuNet boxes the eyes, nose and mouth. `0b1fc684b559cbb8#5` (84%), `7e1167577dd0b80c#0` and `#1` (86%, 83%), and `8422d4529c3e18d3#0` (89.6%, the back of a dark head).
- **2 close-ups in profile, partly covered for the same reason:** `3332ebdcbfa3d3e7#0` and `#1`, a man and a child kissing, 700 and 580 px across (69%, 58%). Their eyes, noses and mouths are inside the blur; the hair and ears aren't.
- **2 aren't people:** `7be102551ef719d6#0`, a face drawn on a sign (18%), and `f4a67e60ad565b34#0`, a face carved in a tree trunk (29%). Open Images didn't mark them as depictions.
- **1 is the back of a head:** `0b1fc684b559cbb8#0` (46%).
- **6 are real faces missed:** `5762f6ddd53543d6#1`, an out-of-focus profile behind flowers (0%); `d35918bf0d93e79c#0`, a face pressed into hair, lying down (0%); `d53d44361e0b2fd2#0`, a face in near darkness at the edge, only its glasses visible (0%); `4e82d116452d8c24#0`, an ear and cheek under a cap at the top edge (0%); `1b85f9ee7207e077#0`, half a face cut by the left edge (62%); and `f6888e526a4c11d0#0`, a 20 px face (17%).

The 3,200 and 2,048 px uploads miss mostly the same faces, plus a few at 85–90% coverage. 2,048 px loses one face entirely: `09bbf6eebbd71c2e#1`, 21 px across.

**Boxes on no annotated face in the Open Images photos** (571 on the originals at 0.3): mostly dogs' faces and fur, wheels, a plush toy, a printed skull, clothing, lanterns, flowers, sand and wood. A few are real background faces that Open Images didn't box (blurred people behind the subject), so its boxes aren't complete for the smallest faces.

**Upload size (issue #41).** 3,200 px keeps recall: 268 faces against 267 for the originals at 0.3, and 33 against 32 of the 37 faces under 40 px at full size. It cuts the median upload from 2.22 to 0.94 MB, and `api`'s median time per photo from 438 to 318 ms (p90 from 1,011 to 401 ms). 2,048 px catches 265, and 30 of the 37 small faces. The gate's blur score moves by 1% or less at the median (ratios 0.999–1.009) and no photo crosses `min_blur_score` 40, because the gate scores a 512 px downscale. The sizes differ by a few faces either way, which this sample can't separate more finely.

## Proposed changes (not made)

1. **`FaceConfig::grow` from 0.25 to 0.35.** Recall 96.1% (+4: the faces at 83–90%). The blur covers 13.3% of an average dev plant photo instead of 11.5%.
2. **A third, whole-image pass at 640 px** (code, not configuration), with growth 0.35: 96.5% (+1, the face in darkness), and 15.1% of an average plant photo blurred. It doesn't fix the close-up profiles.
3. **Not a lower threshold:** 0.15 buys 97.2% with 78% of the plant photos blurred.

Of the 10 faces left after 1 and 2, 2 aren't people, 1 is the back of a head and 2 are close-ups whose eyes, nose and mouth are blurred. The other 5 are real misses.

**The false blur needs more than a setting:** no threshold gets it near 2%. Two things to try: check that a box's five YuNet landmarks (which the model outputs and `api` ignores) are laid out like a face, and say in evaluation 4 whether "a face's box" means the head or the eyes, nose and mouth.

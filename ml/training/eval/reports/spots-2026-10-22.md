# Spot colour thresholds, 2026-10-22

`python -m ml.training.eval.spot_fit fit` (M15; [evaluation 6](../../../../docs/vision/evaluation.md#6-thresholds-set-from-the-evaluation-set)).
The spot segmenter (`ColourSpotSegmenter`, `backend/crates/vision/src/measure/spots.rs`) inside the leaf masks the
perception worker's own models cut (leaf_detector `rfdetr-s-leaf-2026-10-19-bglic`, sam_encoder `sam2.1-hiera-tiny@de431c40`, sam_decoder `sam2.1-hiera-tiny@de431c40`, fp32 on the CPU),
scored against PlantSeg's lesion masks. Fit: PlantSeg's training split; hold-out: its validation and test splits. No value
was chosen on the hold-out: it scores the defaults, the fitted values and each speck share below. It did shape one choice of
method: the lesion-IoU objective's score on it (0.432, below) is why the objective counts the healthy leaf too. Nothing
checks PlantSeg's splits for near-duplicates of each other (`leakage: false` in `sources.yaml`), so the hold-out may share
photos with the fit half (*unverified*).

- **Photos** (`ml/training/data/splits/spots_plantseg.tsv`): fit spots 422, fit areas 271, holdout spots 273, holdout areas 206. A photo is scored
  when one of its leaf masks holds a lesion pixel: fit spots 357, holdout spots 219, fit areas 250, holdout areas 175. No leaf was found on
  fit spots 33, holdout spots 32, fit areas 14, holdout areas 26; 0 photos failed to run.
- **Spots and areas.** PlantSeg's masks outline discrete spots for some diseases (`spots`: rusts, scab, septoria, bacterial
  spot, gray leaf spot, frogeye, black rot, early blight) and paint whole regions for others (`areas`: mildews, late blight,
  leaf mold, northern leaf blight). The thresholds are fitted on `spots` only; `areas` is reported, not fitted.
- **The objective** is the two-class IoU per photo, the mean of the lesion IoU (affected pixels, necrotic or halo, against
  the lesion mask inside the leaf masks) and the healthy leaf's IoU, averaged over photos. A first fit on the lesion IoU
  alone called almost every leaf pixel affected: on these coarse masks that scores the lesion share (0.432 on the
  hold-out), so it rewarded painting the leaf.
- **Necrotic IoU** uses the necrotic pixels alone (a leaf's spots). **Affected-% error:** per leaf, the absolute
  difference between the predicted and the labelled share of the leaf, in points (median / 90th percentile).
- PlantSeg labels lesions without telling a halo apart, so the halo fields are fitted only through the affected pixels.
- **Constraints:** brown's hues stay below the halo's (`brown_hue_max` ≤ `halo_hue_min`), so a spot's size doesn't take
  in its halo, and `dark_value` is searched from 0.2, so a black pixel stays necrotic. Free, the fit turns the dark rule
  off (shadows in field photos) and a black pixel with a yellow tint becomes halo; the floor cost 0.001 of the
  two-class IoU.
- **Five fields end at the edge of their range** (below): `brown_hue_min` 0, `brown_saturation` 0, `pale_value` 0 and
  `halo_saturation` 0 are the ends of their scales, so those conditions are off, and `brown_value` 2.5 is off once the
  leaf's median value is at least 0.4. So every hue of 0–45° darker than 2.5 × the leaf's median value is necrotic, greys
  included (their hue is 0); every pixel under 0.08 saturation is necrotic, white glare included, and a large patch of
  glare outlasts the speck filter; and every hue of 45–66° with 0.08 saturation or more is halo, yellow-green leaf
  included. A wider range can't move the four at the end of their scale; `brown_value` above 2.5 would matter only on
  leaves darker than 0.4, and reds past 0° need a range that wraps, which `SpotThresholds` can't express. Not tried.

## Specks and clean photos (`min_spot_share`)

`python -m ml.training.eval.spot_fit clean-masks` then `clean`. The measurement counts a photo as evidence of health only
when no measured leaf has a spot or a hole ([contracts 7](../../../../docs/architecture/contracts.md#7-pipeline-records),
`clean_images`), and every necrotic patch counts as a spot, one pixel or more. So a speck of glare or grain on a healthy leaf
keeps a photo from reading clean. The segmenter now turns a necrotic patch (4-connected) smaller than `min_spot_share` of
its leaf's pixels into leaf; a share rather than a pixel count, so it holds at any resolution. It works per patch, as the
spot count does, so a lesion broken into pieces can lose its small pieces; at the chosen share it removes nothing on a
leaf under 50,000 px, where the share is a pixel or less. Yellow pixels around a dropped speck stay halo and
still count toward `affected_pct`.

- **Choice:** the largest share on the grid at which PlantSeg's fit-half `spots` photos read clean at most
  1 point more often than with no filter. Every other column only scores it.
- **Photos:** PlantSeg as above (357 fit and 219 hold-out
  `spots`, 175 hold-out `areas` photos with a lesion pixel in a leaf mask); and the check
  photos of `ml/training/data/splits/spots_clean.tsv` at the stored size of a photo with depth (long side 2,048 px), through the same leaf
  masks: 114 healthy (the dev cases' healthy photos and PlantDoc's healthy apple, pepper, grape and tomato
  leaves), 82 dev photos with a spot disease and 15 with an area disease.
  The dev photos are those of `ml/training/eval/manifest.jsonl` (SHA-256 `e48d59e7852da853…`, 102 dev cases, 93 of them healthy or with a spot or area disease), run on 2026-10-23.
- **Clean %** is the share of photos whose every leaf has no spot; for diseased photos it is wrong, for healthy ones right.
  *With holes* also needs no hole in any leaf, as the contract's rule does, so it is the share that would count as
  evidence of health. The IoU is the hold-out two-class IoU with the filter.
- Healthy photos with no spot, no filter → chosen share (with no hole either, as the rule needs): dev 11.8% → 23.5% (5.9% → 5.9%; 17 photos); plantdoc:test 24.3% → 40.5% (8.1% → 10.8%; 37 photos); plantdoc:train 6.7% → 8.3% (0.0% → 1.7%; 60 photos).
- **Holes now keep more healthy photos from reading clean than spots do.** The 405 healthy leaf masks have 1582 holes, median 8 px (90th percentile 84.9 px), 1107 of them 20 px or less, which points to gaps in SAM's masks rather than holes in the leaves (from their sizes; not inspected one by one). Photos with no hole in any leaf: 27 of 114; 46 if holes under 0.01% of their leaf were ignored, 81 under 0.1%. Holes count at any size in the contract, so this is left to a change of the contract or of SAM's post-processing (filling small holes, as its own mask generator does).

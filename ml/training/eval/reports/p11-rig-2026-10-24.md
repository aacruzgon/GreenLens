# P11 on the rig: `findings@6` on Qwen3.5 and FP8 (2026-10-24)

Task P11 ([dev-roadmap](../../../../docs/vision/dev-roadmap.md)) is disease reported as animal damage. `findings@6` ([#213](https://github.com/aacruzgon/GreenLens/pull/213), [vlm.md 7](../../../../docs/vision/vlm.md#findings-call--findings6)) has each damage finding name, in `trace`, the animal seen or the trace it left, and the worker's decode drops a finding whose trace names neither. P11 is done when, on vlm.md 4's seven photos and the dev cases, Qwen3.5 reports no damage finding for a disease sign and P5's checks still hold. These runs are steps 3 and 4 of [#216](https://github.com/aacruzgon/GreenLens/issues/216).

**P11 isn't met yet, but the rule removes most of it.** On the 120 dev cases Qwen3.5 wrote 143 damage findings and decode kept 19. Of the 124 it dropped, 90 traces describe a disease sign ("necrotic spots", "tissue death"). What gets through is "frass": Qwen3.5 writes it, an allowed trace, for small dark specks. It does this on the seven photos' early-blight tomato leaf and on 4 dev cases with a disease or a deficiency. FP8 keeps 95 of its 130, and 62 of those are traced "aphid mummy", the name the prompt's organisms section gives. P5's conditions hold on Qwen3.5.

## Damage findings before and after decode

`p11_damage.py` ports decode's trace rule to Python. On both bench runs its kept count equals the damage the worker decoded on all 120 cases, so the two rules agree. "A disease sign" is the script's flag on a dropped trace (a word such as spot, lesion, necrotic, yellow or tissue). It's for counting only and isn't part of the worker's rule. "With an animal" means the case's truth has a seed problem of `kind: pest` (hornworms, spider mites) or an unboxed organism: 11 of the 120.

## `dscn3175`: why S4's scans said Septoria

On S4's three scans of this photo, Qwen3.5 answered Septoria's three indicators "yes" ([#222](https://github.com/aacruzgon/GreenLens/pull/222)). Here, decode drops all of the tomato's 10 damage findings (each traced "necrotic spots"), and FP8's one ("spots on leaves"). The fixture's questions are answered once in the analyse call (leaf and plant) and once in the verify call (a new photo):

|---|---|---|
| Early blight: rings like a target | yes / no | yes / yes |
| Early blight: mostly on the lowest leaves | no / no | yes / yes |
| Early blight: a yellow ring around the spots | yes / yes | yes / yes |
| Septoria: small, pinhead size | no / no | yes / yes |
| Septoria: pale centres with tiny black specks | no / no | yes / yes |
| Septoria: dozens of spots per leaf | no / no | no / no |
| Late blight: large wet-looking patches | no / no | no / no |
| Late blight: white mould underneath | unsure / unsure | unsure / unsure |
| Late blight: dark stem lesions | no / no | yes / no |

On these questions, Qwen3.5 sees early blight's halo and, once, its rings, and none of Septoria's signs. FP8 says yes to most signs of both. So this recording doesn't reproduce the scans' Septoria answers. The scans asked the seed's own questions (for example "Are there tiny black dots inside the pale centre of the spots?"), on Alan's crop of the leaf. The API doesn't expose the scan's prompt, so the two can't be compared call by call. The tomato's other numbers on this photo are 0 soil readings, 27 reasons up to 81 characters, and all of them end with a full stop.

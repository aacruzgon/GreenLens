- **keep_foliage_dry** (cultural, merged): Keep the leaves dry. Water at the base of the plants, for example with drip or furrow irrigation, rather than from above. - **remove_crop_debris** (cultural, merged): At the end of the season, pull out the old tomato plants and remove as much plant debris as you can from the garden; burn it or throw it away.

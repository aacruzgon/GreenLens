Prevalence set to common because an excerpt calls Bemisia a major pest of tomato, though mostly in warm southern regions and greenhouses. Insect sizes (adult about 1 mm, eggs and nymphs smaller) are not a measurable quantity, so no measure was given. The 'where' for yellow feeding spots, honeydew and sooty mould is my guess (upper_leaves); excerpts do not say which leaves. No temperature or humidity range that favours whiteflies is given. No excerpt compares whiteflies with the listed problems; whiteflies spread tomato yellow leaf curl virus, but that is a cause link, not a look-alike, so looks_like is empty. Virus-only steps (rogue and bag infected plants, resistant TYLCV varieties, living mulches) were left out; end-of-season crop removal was kept because it cuts hosts between crops. Neonicotinoid and diamide insecticides are mentioned only for commercial growers and were left out. No tomato varieties resist whiteflies themselves. Insecticidal soap, oils and the fungal biocontrols are marked not broad-spectrum because no excerpt says they kill beneficials; the reviewer may want to check this, especially for Beauveria bassiana ('effective against Bemisia and other insects'). The oil-spray dilution and weekly interval in the excerpt were omitted.

## Changes after review (2026-10-20)

- **Point 22, `soil_ph`:** the 0 to 14 placeholder is replaced by 6.0 to 6.8 from a new source, `isu_growing_tomatoes_home_garden` (Iowa State University Extension and Outreach, Yard and Garden, "Growing Tomatoes in the Home Garden", Aaron Steil; added to `sources.yaml`, facts only, no chunks). The page gives 6.0 to 6.8, slightly acidic, and says tomatoes also grow in slightly alkaline soil (its wording isn't committed: read it at the page's URL in sources.yaml). The notes add the slightly alkaline point and the deep, loamy, well-drained soil from the same page.
- **Point 22, `spacing_cm`:** set to 90. `clemson_hgic_tomato_diseases.18` (facts only) says to space tomato plants 3 feet (about 90 cm) apart for air circulation and faster drying of the leaves; that is the purpose of the crowding rule that reads this field. The sources differ: `isu_growing_tomatoes_home_garden` gives spacing by training, staked indeterminate and determinate plants 1.5 to 2 feet (45 to 60 cm), caged 2 to 3 feet (60 to 90 cm), sprawling 3 to 4 feet (90 to 120 cm), rows 4 to 5 feet (120 to 150 cm). The notes give both. With 90, staked plants at 45 to 60 cm will be called crowded; choose 60 instead if that is not wanted.
- **Point 22, heat-tolerant cultivars:** kept as the source gives it (the list was already exactly `clemson_hgic_tomato_diseases.46`'s nine names, nothing to trim) and now labelled as Clemson Extension's (South Carolina) suggestion. Kept rather than dropped because it is the only cultivar advice for the 32 °C blossom-drop problem, and the label tells a Canadian gardener where it comes from.
- **`water` (model note asked to confirm):** `medium` now has support: `isu_growing_tomatoes_home_garden` says tomatoes, like most vegetables, do best with one inch (about 2.5 cm) of water a week. The amount is not put in the notes (no amounts to apply); the notes add its morning, at-the-soil watering and that sandy soil needs water more often but less each time. `clemson_hgic_tomato_diseases.18` (facts only) also says to water at the base, not overhead.
- **`light`:** `full_sun` also supported by `isu_growing_tomatoes_home_garden`: at least six hours of direct sun daily for best yields.
- **Not filled:** `mature_height_cm`. Neither the excerpts nor `isu_growing_tomatoes_home_garden` give a mature height.
- **Notes reordered** (soil, pH, spacing, sun, water, temperature, cultivars, nitrogen, rotation, seed); the Clemson facts (`clemson_hgic_tomato_diseases.1`, `.2`, `.46`) are unchanged in substance. `sources` adds `isu_growing_tomatoes_home_garden`.
